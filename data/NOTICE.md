— <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>.

- **`spinnennetz-source.json` / `spinnennetz.geojson`** — places, routes,
  persons and glosses compiled from the public-domain text of Joseph Roth,
  *Das Spinnennetz* (1923). Quotations are verbatim from that text.
  Placements of places the novel does not name (interiors, unnamed stations,
  the fictional estate Lukscha) are **hypothetical** and flagged
  `confidence: low`.

## Sources of coordinates & routes

Coordinates were obtained by geocoding via **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim** and
corrected by hand where a historical site differs from today's (e.g. the
Siegessäule on the Königsplatz until 1939, the former police headquarters at
Alexanderplatz). Routes are drawn with **BRouter**. All coordinates and routes
derive from **OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL** —
<https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.
